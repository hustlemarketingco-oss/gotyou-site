<p><strong>Reclaiming the Power of Consumer Data: How GOTYOU Builds a Better Community</strong></p>

<p>Every time we shop at a local store, grab coffee, or dine out, we generate valuable data. Yet today, this data is siphoned off by tech giants like Google, Facebook, and DoorDash. They monetize it for their gain—selling ads, driving profits, and taking the value out of the communities where it originates. This broken system leaves local businesses disconnected from their customers and consumers with little to show for their contributions.</p>

<p>At GOTYOU, we’re flipping this model on its head.</p>

<h3><strong>Empowering Consumers and Businesses Together</strong></h3>

<p>GOTYOU is building a platform where consumers and businesses retain the value of their interactions. Through simple tools like <strong>check-ins, feedback, referrals, and surveys</strong>, we’re collecting data that rivals—and will one day surpass—what Big Tech gathers. But here’s the difference: instead of enriching distant tech monopolies, our platform is designed to benefit the <strong>community</strong>, not corporations.</p>

<p>When consumers participate on GOTYOU, they’re not just users; they’re <strong>partners in the system</strong>. They earn real rewards for their actions, from sharing feedback to answering surveys, while businesses get direct insights to enhance their services. This creates a sustainable cycle of value that stays local, helping merchants and customers thrive together.</p>

<h3><strong>Building the World’s Largest Local Action Data Set</strong></h3>

<p>Our innovative approach enables us to collect and analyze a vast array of <strong>local action data</strong>—what’s happening in your neighborhood, in real-time. Every check-in, review, referral, and survey adds a meaningful piece to the puzzle, helping businesses better understand their customers. This is the type of granular data that Big Tech monopolizes to dominate the digital advertising world, but we’re putting it to work for local communities instead.</p>

<h3><strong>AI Designed to Serve Communities</strong></h3>

<p>The AI models behind GOTYOU are not designed to maximize ad revenue for a tech company; they’re designed to improve the <strong>user experience</strong> for both consumers and businesses. Our AI learns from local data to:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Deliver personalized recommendations</strong> to users, helping them discover great local spots.</li>

<li><strong>Empower merchants</strong> with actionable insights about their customers.</li>

<li><strong>Strengthen the local economy</strong> by fostering deeper connections between consumers and the businesses they love.</li>
</ul>

<p>Unlike traditional advertising, where consumers remain invisible to merchants, GOTYOU creates a direct, transparent relationship. Businesses can understand their customers, tailor their offers, and build loyalty—all without the need for an expensive middleman.</p>

<h3><strong>Keeping Value Where It Belongs: In Our Community</strong></h3>

<p>GOTYOU’s platform isn’t just a rewards app; it’s a movement to <strong>reclaim the power of data</strong> for the people and places that generate it. By enabling communities to own and benefit from their own data, we’re creating a system that’s more equitable, more effective, and more human.</p>

<p>Our vision is bold: to build the <strong>largest collection of local action data in the world</strong>. But more importantly, we want this data to work for you—the consumer—and for the businesses in your community. Together, we can create a future where every dollar, every interaction, and every insight helps build stronger, more connected local economies.</p>

<p>With GOTYOU, the power of data stays in your hands—and in your community.</p>
