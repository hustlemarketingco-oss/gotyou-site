<p>Groupon showed us the power of digital coupons. It was a brilliant idea that exploded into billions in revenue almost overnight. But it came at a cost. Small businesses got squeezed, losing margin and control. What looked like growth was often a slow bleed for local merchants.</p>

<p>It is time for something better. Something that creates real value for consumers and merchants, and keeps more money in our local communities instead of in the pockets of Big Tech.</p>

<h2>Local Commerce Runs on Convenience</h2>

<p>E-commerce is built on convenience. I can click, buy, and have it delivered. Underneath it all are discounts, coupons, free shipping, and most importantly, data. Data is the fuel that drives personalized offers and repeat sales.</p>

<p>Local commerce has the same foundation: convenience, what is near me, and deals. But the tools are broken. Billions of paper coupons are floating around, and none of them create a connection between the business and the consumer. At best, there is a code in a POS system. But try remarketing to a paper coupon code. It does not work.</p>

<p>That was Groupon’s problem as well. No connection. Businesses gave up margin, customers got a one-time deal, and nothing lasting came from it.</p>

<h2>Why This Works: The Science of Offers</h2>

<p>Science shows that offers drive transactions. Always have, always will.</p>

<ul>
<li>In 1887, Coca-Cola launched the first coupon for a free drink. Within a decade, Coke was in every state. The free offer created a habit, drove adoption, and built a household name.</li>

<li>Studies show that even small vouchers make people happier, less stressed, and more loyal.</li>

<li>Free offers consistently beat discounts. In one study, shoppers bought 73 percent more when they received a free bonus instead of a price cut.</li>

<li>Retailers have seen free samples boost sales by as much as 2,000 percent.</li>
</ul>

<p>The lesson is simple. Offers create action. They build connections. They change behavior. Paper coupons and Groupon failed because they created no connection. GOTYOU solves that.</p>

<h2>From Coca-Cola to TikTok</h2>

<p>The same principle that fueled Coca-Cola over a century ago is driving the biggest platforms today. TikTok now generates more revenue than Meta, powered not just by content, but by deals and offers that consumers act on immediately. Offers are timeless. They worked in pharmacies in the 1880s. They work on TikTok today.</p>
