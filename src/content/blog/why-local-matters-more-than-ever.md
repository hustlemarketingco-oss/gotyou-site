<p><em>On the road with GOTYOU, Summer 2025</em></p>

<p>We’re four weeks in. Ten of us. One mission: strengthen local communities.</p>

<p>Since June 1, we’ve walked into more than 1,000 local businesses across Florida, Georgia, the Carolinas, and Virginia. We&#8217;ve had thousands of conversations with everyday people—owners, workers, neighbors.</p>

<p>We’ve logged over 2.5 million steps. In the heat. In the rain. On sidewalks, in parking lots, and through every kind of Main Street.</p>

<p>This isn&#8217;t a remote campaign. It&#8217;s not theory. It&#8217;s not some strategy we’re drafting in a quiet office. This is real life—on the ground, face-to-face, business by business.</p>

<p>The journey started in St. George, Utah. After a fast three-day drive across the country, we met the team we’d spend the summer with. And then we got to work.</p>

<p>We’ve launched GOTYOU in 10 small zones—from South Florida to just outside the White House. We’re connecting people to the businesses that keep their communities going. And we&#8217;re rewarding them for it. Not with points. With real cash. Real value.</p>

<p>Local businesses are the backbone of this country. But they’re being squeezed—by costs, by middlemen, by platforms that take more than they give.</p>

<p>GOTYOU was built to push back. To cut Big Tech out of the middle. To make it easier—and worth it—for people to shop local.</p>

<p>And it’s working. The response we’ve seen proves something we already knew:<br><strong>People want to support local. They just need a better way to do it.</strong></p>

<p>We’re building that way. One step at a time.</p>
