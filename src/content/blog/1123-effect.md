In one of the toughest consumer economies in a generation, local businesses are discovering something remarkable: the moment a customer decides to return isn't when they leave happy — it's earlier than that, and it's measurable.

## What the data shows

Across thousands of check-ins, a pattern emerges around the timing of the first follow-up touch after a visit. Businesses that reach customers within that window see meaningfully higher repeat-visit rates than those who don't.

## Why this matters for local commerce

Big-box retailers have run this playbook for years with abandoned-cart emails and loyalty triggers. Local businesses, until now, haven't had access to the same tooling — not because the insight doesn't apply to them, but because nobody built the infrastructure for a coffee shop or a plumber to act on it.

That's the gap GOTYOU exists to close.
