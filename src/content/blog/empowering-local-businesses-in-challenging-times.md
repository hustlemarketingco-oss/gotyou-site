<p>For the restaurant industry, the effects are already being felt. According to a June 2024 National Restaurant Association report, restaurant sales have softened, and real sales have dropped to their lowest levels since April 2023, despite menu prices continuing to rise. U.S. consumers, who largely brushed off rising interest rates post-pandemic, are now showing signs of restraint. This leaves local businesses with a critical challenge: how to connect with consumers in a meaningful way that drives sales without relying solely on deep discounts that hurt profitability.</p>

<h3>Inflation and Consumer Behavior: Navigating the Changing Economy</h3>

<p>As inflation persists, consumers are stretching every dollar. A recent Deloitte report confirms that consumers are adopting more frugal behaviors, from trading down to lower-priced products to cutting back on discretionary spending. This has forced local businesses to rethink their approach to marketing and customer engagement.</p>

<p>Discounting, once a strategic tool used sparingly, has become a necessary tactic for survival. Just take a look at <strong>Groupon’s success</strong> during the 2008-2012 recession—it was one of the fastest-growing companies during that period, fueled by consumers looking for discounts and businesses needing a way to drive traffic. But discounting can be a double-edged sword. Offering broad, untargeted discounts may get people through the door, but it can erode profits without guaranteeing long-term customer loyalty.</p>

<p>That’s why businesses need to be smarter with their promotions. With GOTYOU’s platform, local merchants can offer personalized discounts that are tailored to the customer’s preferences and behaviors. These targeted offers help ensure that promotions drive repeat business and build lasting relationships, rather than just being a one-time sale.</p>
