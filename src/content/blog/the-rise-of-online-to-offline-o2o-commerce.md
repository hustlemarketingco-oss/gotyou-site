<p>I&#8217;ll start by asking the big question: What the heck is online-to-offline (O2O) commerce?</p>

<p>The explanation is relatively straightforward. O2O commerce refers to the process of promoting your product online to attract customers and encourage them to make purchases in physical stores. This involves digital advertising on social media platforms such as Facebook and TikTok.</p>

<p>Over the last decade, digital advertising has become the go-to for not just small businesses, but those of all sizes. It&#8217;s no secret that over <a href="https://expertbeacon.com/social-media-business-statistics/">90% of enterprises</a> prioritize social media in their business goals.</p>

<p>Now, more than ever, small businesses are turning to social media to market their products. The presence of social media provides companies with an easy avenue to sell their product.</p>

<hr/>

<h2>Price Hikes 💸</h2>

<p>Recently, however, it has become increasingly complex for smaller businesses to afford digital advertising. Let&#8217;s break this down into numbers:</p>

<ul>
<li>Over the last year, the cost of a single <strong>Google </strong>search ad increased by over <strong><em>5%</em></strong> from 2024 to 2025.
<ul>
<li>Worse still, there was a <strong><em>24% </em></strong>cost increase from 2023 to 2024.</li>
</ul>
</li>

<li>The cost of digital advertising on platforms such as Facebook, TikTok, and Snapchat is measured by the cost per thousand impressions (CPM). This is the amount of money an advertiser pays for every 1,000 impressions their advertisement gets.
<ul>
<li>In 2022, <a href="https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbescommunicationscouncil/2022/11/14/how-marketers-are-fighting-rising-ad-costs/"><strong>Facebook&#8217;s </strong>ad costs</a> increased by <strong><em>89%</em></strong>.</li>

<li><strong>TikTok </strong>CPM has<strong> </strong>increased by <strong><em>92%.</em></strong></li>

<li><strong>Snapchat </strong>CPM has increased by <strong><em>64%.</em></strong></li>

<li>In total, advertising costs have increased by <strong>222%</strong> over the last decade.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<p>Several factors contribute to the rising cost of digital advertising. These include increases in <strong>data costs</strong>, which make it more expensive for companies to both produce and sustain their advertisements. There&#8217;s also Apple&#8217;s <strong>App Tracking Transparency </strong>feature, a policy that allows consumers to opt out of certain apps collecting and selling their data. This has led to a decrease in the collection and sale of consumer data, which has significantly impacted advertising revenue for major platforms.</p>

<p>What&#8217;s perhaps been the most impactful is the sudden <strong>increase in demand</strong> for digital advertising space. This has been especially boosted primarily due to the COVID-19 pandemic, which forced companies to close their doors and market from a distance.</p>

<p>While larger corporations could manage marketing from home, smaller businesses crumbled, according to information gained from a Harvard University-based project known as <a href="https://www.tracktherecovery.org/">The Economic Tracker</a>. The tracker states that a resounding 37.5% of small businesses in the United States closed between January 2020 and June 2021.</p>

<p>Why does this matter? It proves that, in a tech-heavy world filled with online markets, a vast and vital number of sales continue to be made in person. However, in a day and age where demand is high, digital ad engagement is low, and advertising is expensive, how can a small business stand out from the others?</p>
