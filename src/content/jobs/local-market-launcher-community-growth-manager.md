<p><strong>Location:</strong> ANY | <strong>Department:</strong> Growth &amp; Expansion | <strong>Type:</strong> Full-Time</p>

<h3>Help Us Build the Future of Local Commerce 🚀</h3>

<p>GOTYOU is a two-sided marketplace that connects local businesses and consumers through exclusive rewards, gift card offers, and community-driven perks — all powered by blockchain-based incentives.</p>

<p>We’re on a mission to help people shop, eat, and engage locally — and get rewarded for it. As we expand across the country, we’re looking for <strong>City Growth Managers</strong> to launch and grow GOTYOU in key markets.</p>

<hr/>

<h3>What You’ll Do</h3>

<p>As a City Growth Manager, you’ll own growth in your city from the ground up. That means acquiring and supporting local merchants, growing and engaging users, and activating the community. You’ll also build and lead a small team of Brand Ambassadors and Sales Support as your market scales.</p>

<ul>
<li>Lead local merchant acquisition, onboarding, and support</li>

<li>Drive user growth through grassroots marketing and community campaigns</li>

<li>Build brand awareness through local partnerships and gifting initiatives</li>

<li>Launch local campaigns that boost app check-ins, gift card redemptions, and engagement</li>

<li>Hire and manage Brand Ambassadors and Sales Support as you hit growth milestones</li>

<li>Create partnerships with chambers, influencers, and local organizations</li>

<li>Own market-level KPIs and report on progress and insights</li>

<li>Represent GOTYOU in your city and bring our mission to life on the ground</li>
</ul>

<hr/>

</ul>
