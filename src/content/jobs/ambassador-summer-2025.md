<h2>About the Role</h2>

<p>This summer, GOTYOU is hitting the road to bring life back to Main Street.</p>

<p>We’re launching GOTYOU — a new kind of local rewards and business platform — in 60 cities across America in 60 days. Our mission is simple: connect small businesses with new customers, drive foot traffic into local stores, and create content that celebrates what makes every city unique.</p>

<p>We’re building two travel teams to lead the charge — and we’re looking for outgoing, creative, hard-working people who want to be part of something bigger.</p>

<li>Connect customers with local businesses — help drive 50-100 new customers into small businesses in every city</li>

<li>Create daily video content — vlogs, reels, interviews, behind-the-scenes stories</li>

<li>Tell local stories — feature the people, places, and small businesses that make each city special</li>

<li>Drive app downloads and engagement — promote GOTYOU’s offers, rewards, and community features</li>

<li>Travel cross-country — stay in hotels, rep GOTYOU gear, and be part of a high-energy, all-in team</li>
</ul>
