<h2>Help Small Businesses Tell Big Stories</h2>

<p>GOTYOU is redefining local advertising — and we need a sharp, organized, and creative <strong>Content Editor</strong> to help us bring our merchant partners&#8217; stories to life.</p>

<p>You’ll work directly with local businesses across the country to turn their GOTYOU campaigns into engaging, high-performing content. That includes editing short-form video, writing clean and compelling copy, optimizing visuals, and coordinating across our team and theirs.</p>

<hr/>

<h2>About GOTYOU</h2>

<p>We’re a performance-based platform helping small businesses win back control of their customer relationships. As we roll out our <strong>60-city Shop Local Tour</strong> this summer, we’re creating campaigns, offers, and loyalty tools with real merchants in real neighborhoods — and every one of them needs great content to succeed.</p>

<hr/>

<h2>What You’ll Do</h2>

<ul>
<li>Work with merchants to edit and format content for their GOTYOU campaigns (offers, vouchers, social, etc.)</li>

<li>Edit short-form video for Reels, TikTok, IG, and YouTube Shorts</li>

<li>Write, revise, and polish captions, headlines, and in-app messaging</li>

<li>Review user-submitted content and elevate it to brand standard</li>

<li>Build and maintain templates, asset libraries, and internal style guides</li>

<li>Collaborate with the social, product, and ambassador teams on content needs for each market</li>

<li>Help standardize campaign content across verticals (restaurants, retail, entertainment, etc.)</li>

<li>Assist in onboarding new merchants by helping them launch strong content in their first 30 days</li>
</ul>

<hr/>

<h2>You’re a Great Fit If You…</h2>

<ul>
<li>Have 2+ years experience editing content (social, brand, product, or media)</li>

<li>Are confident in basic video editing tools (CapCut, InShot, Premiere Rush, etc.)</li>

<li>Can write tight, platform-native copy across channels (IG, TikTok, app messaging)</li>

<li>Are highly organized, deadline-driven, and customer-focused</li>

<li>Can balance brand consistency with merchant personality and local flavor</li>

<li>Are excited to work with real people and help them grow their businesses</li>

<li>Know how to scale quality without reinventing the wheel for every post</li>

<li>Bonus: experience in marketing, e-comm, or content for SMBs</li>
</ul>
